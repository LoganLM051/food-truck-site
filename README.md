# food-truck-site

## What changes at each breakpoint

- Phones (375): Cards and boxes are both stacked. Heading sizes are of their initial sizes
- From md (768px): Cards and boxes are side by side. Headings are larger than the initial size
- From lg (992px): The cards are in 3 columns, the box are still side by side but spaced side by side, and headings are further increased in font-size.
